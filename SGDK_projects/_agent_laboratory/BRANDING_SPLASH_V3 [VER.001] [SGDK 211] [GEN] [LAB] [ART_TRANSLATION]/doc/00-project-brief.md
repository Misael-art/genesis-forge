# Project Brief — BRANDING_SPLASH_V3

## Goal

Build an isolated SGDK 2.11 laboratory prototype for a single blue-energy splash: black and silence, particles expanding and converging, the “SGDK” wordmark filling, “FORGE” appearing beneath it, a left-to-right glow sweep, then a clean fade into the menu.

## Success

- Storyboard and scanline choreography are declared before final pixels.
- The 24, 32, and 48 particle steps are measured before the density is locked.
- Every promoted image and audio resource has provenance and a validated hardware contract.
- The scene builds through the central wrapper and is observed in BlastEm with evidence bound to the ROM hash.

## Scope

This project is a `technical_demo` in `_agent_laboratory`; its delivery claim ceiling is `technical_demo`. It does not claim a complete game or `ready_for_aaa`. Template promotion and each consumer migration remain separate approval gates.

## Non-goals

- Do not change any of the five named consumer projects during the lab.
- Do not archive the template's current splash until the v3 candidate is reviewed for promotion.
- Do not draw final lettering or scenery with runtime/Python primitives.
